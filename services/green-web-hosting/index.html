Why green

Hosting is the easiest sustainability win your website has.

  1. The internet has a carbon footprint

    Data centres and transmission networks now account for around 1–2% of global electricity use. Hosting choice is one of the highest-leverage sustainability decisions any business can make for its digital footprint.

  2. Green hosting is verifiable

    We host with providers on The Green Web Foundation's verified directory — not just companies that buy offsets and call it neutral. You can independently check whether a site is hosted green at thegreenwebfoundation.org.

  3. It is no slower, and no more expensive

    Modern renewable-powered UK hosting performs identically to fossil-fuel-backed grid hosting on speed, uptime, and cost. The trade-off is essentially zero — which makes the case for switching very straightforward.

Frequently asked

Green hosting questions we hear a lot.

Reading up first? Our green web hosting comparison covers the main UK providers in depth.

What is managed green web hosting?
Managed green web hosting is website hosting that runs on verified renewable energy and is fully looked after for you. With OakFox, your site sits in a UK data centre on infrastructure listed in The Green Web Foundation's verified directory, and we handle setup, DNS, SSL, daily backups, security updates, and monitoring — so you never log into a control panel. Plans start at £9 a month and include free migration from your current host.
Is green web hosting actually greener, or is it marketing?
Both exist. Genuinely green hosting runs on verified renewable energy — solar, wind, hydro — and can be independently checked against The Green Web Foundation's public directory. Hosts that simply purchase carbon offsets to claim "neutrality" are not the same thing. OakFox only resells hosts on the verified green directory.
Where are your servers located?
All hosting plans run in UK data centres — relevant for GDPR, for performance to UK visitors, and for the carbon intensity of the underlying grid.
Will moving to green hosting hurt my SEO or speed?
No. Modern green hosting performs identically to standard hosting on every metric — uptime, response time, Core Web Vitals, Lighthouse score. If anything, a move from a slow shared host to managed green hosting often improves performance.
Do you handle the migration from my current host?
Yes, free on every plan. We move your files, databases, email accounts, and DNS records, then schedule the DNS cutover for a quiet time so visitors see zero downtime. Most migrations complete inside a working day.
What is included in "managed"?
Setup, DNS configuration, SSL certificates, automated backups, security updates, uptime monitoring, and a real human to email when something is wrong. You never need to log into cPanel unless you want to.
Do you offer green web hosting in London / Manchester / Liverpool / elsewhere in the UK?
Yes — every plan is available UK-wide. Hosting is location-independent at the point of purchase, though we serve clients across the UK including London, Manchester, Liverpool, Birmingham, Leeds, Edinburgh, and our home patch of West Lancashire / Ormskirk.
What happens if I want to leave?
You own your data. We hand over a complete export — files, databases, DNS records — and assist with migration to your new host. No lock-in, no exit fees, no awkward conversations.
How does this compare to Krystal, Kualo, or GreenGeeks directly?
Going direct to a host is cheaper if you are comfortable managing your own hosting. OakFox's plans cost a little more because they include hands-on UK support, free migrations, ongoing maintenance, and the same person to talk to whether the question is about hosting, your site, or your brand.
