Five stages. Honest measurement.

  1. 01

    Listen

    A conversation about the business, the customers you already have, the goals for the next 6 to 12 months, and what marketing has done so far — what worked, what did not, and what never got tried.

  2. 02

    Audit

    A focused look at your current presence: website, analytics, organic rankings, social, email list. We find where the leverage is, not where the vanity metrics look good.

  3. 03

    Strategy

    A one-document plan: audiences, channels, content pillars, editorial calendar, KPIs. Agreed with you before anything gets built.

  4. 04

    Build

    Delivery — writing, designing, shipping. Content calendar populated. Email sequences written. Organic social moving. SEO improvements committed.

  5. 05

    Review

    Monthly check-ins against the KPIs we agreed. Honest read on what is working, what to cut, what to double down on. No bloated reports — just what changed and why.

Marketing questions we hear a lot.

How much does a marketing retainer cost?
Marketing engagements at OakFox typically run from £1,500/month for focused content + SEO to £6,000+/month for strategy, content, email, and organic social in combination. One-off strategy engagements start at £3,500. Every proposal is scoped to the hours and services you actually need rather than a fixed tier.
Do you run paid ads?
Only when it is the honest answer. Paid media works for specific scenarios — product launches, seasonal campaigns, event promotion, geographic expansion. For most independent brands, organic content, SEO, and email deliver better long-term returns per pound. We will tell you which category you fall into before taking your budget.
Can you handle SEO for a new website?
Yes. SEO for OakFox means clear information architecture, substantive content that answers real queries, fast page performance, proper structured data, and steady content development around topical authority. No link schemes, no programmatic junk, no AI-generated filler. Results take three to nine months; the compounding starts immediately.
Will my content be written by AI?
No. We use AI for research, first drafts on internal documents, and efficiency on administrative tasks — we will not ship it as client-facing work. Every published piece is written, edited, and fact-checked by a human who understands your business. Substance over volume.
How do you measure marketing success?
Honestly. We agree the two or three metrics that actually matter for your business — qualified enquiries, direct traffic, organic rankings for target queries, email list growth, retention — and report against them monthly. No bloated dashboards, no vanity metrics. If something is not working we cut it and say so.
What sectors do you work with?
Independent hospitality, small consumer brands, professional services, cultural and charitable organisations, and B2B software — with a particular focus on sustainability-driven businesses. We turn down sectors we cannot support in good faith, which for us usually means fossil fuels, fast fashion, and anything relying on manipulative advertising.
