Tone of Voice

We are

  • Honest and direct — no jargon, no fluff
  • Confident but not arrogant
  • Warm and approachable — small studio energy
  • Knowledgeable — 15+ years shows in how we explain things
  • Sustainability-minded without being preachy
  • Craft-focused — we care about the details

We are not

  • Corporate or stiff — no "synergy" or "leverage"
  • Salesy or pushy — the work speaks for itself
  • Vague about sustainability — specific claims only
  • Trendy for the sake of it — timeless over fashionable
  • Self-deprecating — confident in our ability
  • Overly casual — professional, not matey

Social Media

Imagery

  • Natural light, earthy tones
  • Process shots and behind-the-scenes
  • Real work, not stock photography
  • Subtle grain texture on graphics
  • Forest green as the consistent accent

Graphic Templates

  • Cream (#F5F0E8) background default
  • Forest (#1A5C12) for headlines and accents
  • Playfair Display for hero text
  • Work Sans for supporting copy
  • Logo mark in corner, never centred

Copy Style

  • Lead with insight, not self-promotion
  • One clear point per post
  • Short paragraphs, no walls of text
  • Hashtags: 3–5 max, relevant only
  • CTA on max 1 in 4 posts
