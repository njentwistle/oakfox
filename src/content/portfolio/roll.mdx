Roll partnered with OakFox to build a brand identity from the ground up — covering everything from visual identity and in-store experience to wholesale packaging and sustainability consultancy for their Lake District bakery and coffee shop.

## The Problem

Roll had brilliant products and a loyal local following, but no cohesive brand to match. The visual identity was inconsistent, the in-store experience didn't reflect the craft behind the baking, and they had no wholesale-ready packaging or brand system to support growth beyond the shop floor.

## The Brief

Create a modern brand identity described as "calm, warm, and contemporary" that matched the quality of Roll's craft offerings. The work extended beyond visuals to include supply chain refinement, waste-smart practices, and compostable packaging development.

## The Solution

### Brand Identity

The identity was designed to feel as tactile and handmade as the products themselves — soft, rounded, and warm.

#### Colour Palette

- **Smoky Black** — deep and grounding, used for primary type and high-contrast elements. Gives the brand weight without harshness.
- **Sea Salt White** — clean and fresh, the dominant background tone. Creates breathing room and lets the products take centre stage.
- **Pakistan Green** — natural and earthy, the primary accent. Ties the brand to its Lake District setting and sustainable values.
- **Pale Dogwood** — soft warmth, used for secondary backgrounds and accents. Evokes the blush of fresh pastry and early morning light.
- **Russet** — rich and artisanal, a warm accent for highlights. Suggests freshly baked crust and ground coffee.

#### Typography

**Headings: Arista Alternate 2.0** — chosen for its soft, chunky aesthetic that mirrors the shape of pastries and sourdough loaves. It's playful without being childish, approachable without losing craft credibility.

**Body: Bubblebody** — accessible and easy to read. Its rounded forms complement the heading typeface and reinforce the friendly, welcoming tone of the brand.
