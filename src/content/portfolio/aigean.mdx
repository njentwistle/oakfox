Aigéan Restaurant partnered with OakFox for a complete brand overhaul, marketing strategy, and environmental consultancy — timed to coincide with the arrival of a Michelin-starred chef at their Sandymount, Dublin location.

## The Problem

Aigéan had the food, the location, and the talent — but the brand didn't reflect the quality of what was happening in the kitchen. With a Michelin-starred chef joining the team, the existing identity felt dated and disconnected from the refined coastal dining experience they were delivering. The supply chain was also inefficient, eating into margins.

## The Brief

Create a contemporary brand identity that reflects Aigéan's vibrant coastal setting and elevated dining experience. Beyond the visuals, OakFox was asked to consult on supply chain optimisation and sustainability — connecting the restaurant with local producers to reduce costs while improving quality and margins.

## The Solution

### Brand Identity

The identity draws directly from the Irish coastline — deep navy waters, weathered stone, and the warmth of a candlelit dining room.

![Aigéan full logo](/images/aigean/Full-logo-1-scaled.png)

#### Colour Palette

- **Oxford Blue** — deep, sophisticated navy evoking the Atlantic coast and evening dining
- **Blue Gray** — coastal calm, used for secondary elements and backgrounds
- **Antique White** — warmth and refinement, grounding the palette with an approachable softness
- **Baker-Miller Pink** — a subtle accent adding unexpected warmth, used sparingly for highlights
- **Coffee** — rich and grounding, connecting the brand to the earthy, artisanal quality of the food

#### Typography

**Primary: Cormorant Infant** — a refined serif from the Cormorant family. Chosen for its elegance and readability at display sizes, it brings a sense of occasion without feeling unapproachable. The Infant variant softens the formality, making it feel welcoming.

**Secondary: Shadows Into Light** — a handwritten style reserved for highlighting the Michelin-starred chef's personal touch. It creates a deliberate contrast with the structured serif, suggesting the human craft behind each dish.

### Sustainability Consultancy

![Local fishermen](/images/aigean/fishermenbw-1-scaled.png)

OakFox mapped the entire supply chain and built direct relationships with local suppliers:

- Direct connections with local fishermen and farmers
- Introduction of a local pottery craftswoman for bespoke hand-crafted plates
- Reduced transportation miles and procurement costs
- Kitchen sustainability practices implementation
- Biodegradable packaging recommendations
- Energy-efficient appliance guidance
- Chemical-reduction cleaning protocols

![Oysters](/images/aigean/oysterbw-1-scaled.png)
