Welcome to the New OakFox

OakFox · 4 March 2026
news development astro

We’re thrilled to launch our completely rebuilt website. Built with Astro and Tailwind CSS, our new site is faster, more accessible, and better reflects who we are as a modern digital agency.

Why the Change?

As a web and digital agency, our own site should be a showcase of what’s possible. We wanted something that:

  • Loads instantly — no heavy WordPress overhead
  • Scores perfectly on Lighthouse audits
  • Is easy to maintain — adding new portfolio pieces and blog posts is as simple as writing a markdown file
